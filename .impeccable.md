## Design Context

### Users
Home cooks and health-conscious grocery shoppers, primarily aged 20-40, using the app immediately after grocery shopping or standing in the kitchen deciding what to cook. Context: low-friction, one hand on the phone, wants answers fast. They're motivated by reducing food waste, eating better, and discovering new recipes without the effort of searching.

### Brand Personality
Fresh. Confident. Alive.

Not a food delivery orange. Not a wellness pastel. Think: a serious home chef's mise en place — dark countertop, vivid fresh ingredients, purposeful. The app should feel like picking up a professional kitchen tool, not scrolling an Instagram feed.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Theme**: Dark mode only. Non-negotiable.
**Background**: #0C120E (deep green-tinted black — not pure black, not grey)
**Surface**: #141A15 (elevated surface)
**Accent**: #3DB85A (fresh, confident green — 10% usage rule, never overused)
**Green-tinted neutrals throughout** — every surface has a hint of green in it

**Visual tone**: Immersive, editorial, confident. More "Bon Appétit dark edition" than "DoorDash." Generous whitespace, not cramped. Typography-forward. The food photography and recipe names should feel like they're the hero.

**Anti-references**: Nothing that looks like a generic health/fitness app. No pastel greens. No neon glows. No cyan-on-dark. No purple-to-blue gradients. No glassmorphism used decoratively. No gradient text. No side-stripe borders. No orange CTAs. No identical card grids.

**Style**: The "Modern Dark (Cinema Mobile)" style from ui-ux-pro-max — but grounded with green-tinted neutrals instead of purple/indigo accents. Rich, layered surfaces. Hairline borders (rgba(255,255,255,0.08)). Clean typography hierarchy. The accent green is rare and purposeful.

### Design Principles
1. **Fresh over flashy** — The green tint pervades everything subtly. No neon. No glow. The brand lives in the texture of the surfaces, not in accent decoration.
2. **Typography is the hero** — Recipe names, ingredient lists, and nutrition data should feel like they were typeset by a food magazine designer. Size contrast, weight, and spacing do the heavy lifting.
3. **Purposeful scarcity of color** — Accent green (#3DB85A) is earned. It appears on one CTA per screen, on success states, on match percentages. It does not appear on borders, backgrounds, or decorative elements.
4. **Every number is tabular** — Calories, macros, timers, percentages: all use `fontVariant: ['tabular-nums']`. Numbers should never jump around as they change.
5. **Motion has meaning** — No decorative animations. Enter from where the thing came from. Exit faster than it entered. The app should feel alive but never showy.
